# Polish: rounded cards that zoom, the logo everywhere, screen fades

The third of three PRs that bring the Roku app in line with arc-tv and arc-apple (after #26 and #27).

## What changed

- **Cards** (all rows and grids, via `CardItem`):
  - Rounded corners, 16 px. A `MaskGroup` uses `mask-poster.png` or `mask-thumb.png`;
    arc-tv's corners are 20 px at about 1.4 times the size.
  - A focused card grows 6% and shows a 6 px white rounded ring (`focus-ring.9.png`, a
    nine-patch) just inside its picture, like arc-tv's cards, which grow 8% with an outside ring.
  - The zoom follows `focusPercent`, so it animates as the focus moves along a row.
  - Each card sits 14 px inside its slot (`cardPad`/`cardSlot` in `cards.bs`), and the lists'
    gaps and positions take that room back. The cards land where they did, and the zoom isn't
    clipped at a list's edge.
  - Row headings move 14 px to stay level with the cards.
  - Round cast pictures keep their ring and now zoom too.
- **The logo in the remaining places**, as arc-tv:
  - the mark at the top of the rail, crossfading to the main logo when the rail opens (the
    profile and places move down 46 px to make room);
  - the stacked logo above sign-in;
  - the main logo above "Who's watching?".
- **Screens fade in** over 0.2 s when they open.
- `scripts/make-card-art.mjs` draws the masks and the ring; `scripts/make-logos.mjs` adds
  `logo-main.png` and `logo-mark.png`.

## Decisions

- **No bundled font.** arc-tv uses the TV's own font (LG Smart UI, SamsungOne) and arc-apple
  uses the system font, so the Roku's system font is the match. Arc's brand font, Quicksand, is
  only in the logo.
- **A 6% zoom, not 8%, and the ring inside rather than outside.** Both fit in the 14 px of room
  without the row clipping them.
- **No drop shadow.** Roku can't draw one, so it would need a large blurred image under every
  card. The ring and zoom show the focus clearly.
- **Buttons unchanged.** They already match arc-tv: white Play or Resume with black text, round
  buttons beside it.
- **The player's controls** get their own PR.

## Checks

- `npm run check`: 250 strings, catalog up to date.
- `npm test`: passed.
- `npm run build`: packaged.
- Simulator and real Roku: not run here. The owner tests the sideloaded build.
- Worth watching on a cheaper Roku: every card is now a `MaskGroup`, which costs more to draw
  than a plain picture. If rows stutter on a low-end model, the corners can be dropped there.

## Logging

None. These are visual changes with no new failure paths.
